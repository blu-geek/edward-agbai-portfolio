About

Edward Agbai

Edward Agbai brings 34 years of cross-sector leadership to the work of building institutions, bridging continents, and developing the leaders of tomorrow. His career spans 17 years at the intersection of energy and enterprise, a decade in academia, and seven years in strategic consulting and mentorship — a through-line defined by intellectual depth, principled ambition, and a commitment to lasting impact.

Across three active organizations, Edward operates where policy, research, and organizational development converge. He leads with a distinctly pan-African orientation — advancing partnerships between Canada and the continent while shaping discourse on leadership, contemporary global issues, and the enduring power of mentorship. He is a published author, keynote speaker, and lifelong advocate for learning as the foundation of every successful organization.
